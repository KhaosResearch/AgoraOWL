Ontology Specification Draft

Abstract

Lightweight ontology for data spaces. Version 1.2.0 introduces symmetric profiling for DataApps (Input/Output ports), generalizes administrative metadata for both Resources and Apps, and reinforces the schema-agnostic design of DataSpecifications. The modeling follows alignment with DCAT-AP-ES, the CRED model, and the IDSA Information Model to ensure cross-domain semantic interoperability.

Introduction back to ToC

AgoraOWL provides a compact yet expressive vocabulary to describe the operational semantics of data spaces. Version 1.2.0 consolidates the symmetric profiling model and improves cross-domain metadata management. ### What's new in v1.2.0 - **Symmetric App Profiling**: Introduces `OutputProfile` to complement `InputProfile`, enabling Apps to declare their output capabilities atomically or grouped by ports. - **Generalized Metadata**: Properties such as `hasCertification`, `supportContact`, or `legalContact` can now be applied to both Data Assets (`ids:Resource`) and Applications (`agoraowl:DataApp`). - **Strict Semantic Separation**: Completed the technical decoupling of `DataSpecification`. Schema details (`hasSchemaType`) now reside exclusively in the Distribution/Mapping layer. ### Decoupled Architecture 1. **DataSpecification (Semantics)**: Atomic reusable unit (FOI + Observable Property). 2. **FieldMapping (Bridge)**: Links semantics to physical fields, units, and quality metrics. 3. **Profiles (Requirements)**: `InputProfile` and `OutputProfile` define the semantic requirements/offers of Apps.

AgoraOWL: Description back to ToC

El objetivo principal de AgoraOWL es conseguir la interoperabilidad semántica sectorial en los espacios de datos, desacoplando las distribuciones físicas de los activos de sus especificaciones semánticas. La versión 1.2.0 se ha diseñado siguiendo el alineamiento estratégico con DCAT-AP-ES, el modelo CRED y el Information Model de IDSA, e introduce el perfilado simétrico de aplicaciones (Input/Output Profiles) para un emparejamiento técnico formal. Por ejemplo, una aplicación que requiera 'Humedad del Suelo' en porcentaje puede descubrir y consumir automáticamente un Dataset cuya distribución (CSV/Parquet) esté mapeada semánticamente a dicha especificación mediante FieldMappings.
